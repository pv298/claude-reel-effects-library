# Claude Reel Effects Library

An open, practical library of **120 short-form video editing effects and techniques** for creators, editors, educators and marketers. Use the catalogue as a creative reference and ask Claude to turn selected effects into an editing plan for your footage.

> Independent community resource. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic or Claude.

## Creator websites
- [FSIDM](https://fsidm.in/)
- [Pranav Veerani](https://pranavveerani.com/)

## Browse
Open `index.html` in a browser to search and filter the catalogue. Structured data lives in `effects/effects.json`.

## Use with Claude
1. Select effects from the catalogue.
2. Provide your own footage or describe your clips, platform and editing software.
3. Copy `prompts/claude-reel-editor.md` into Claude.
4. Ask for a timecoded edit decision list, effect settings, sound design and implementation steps.
5. Review and render the edit in your chosen editor.

Claude can help plan edits and produce instructions; this repository does not itself render or export video.

## Categories
Transitions & Cuts · Motion & Camera · Typography & Graphics · Color & Film Looks · Effects & Compositing · Audio & Rhythm

## Quality and rights
Use effects to support the story, keep captions mobile-readable, protect dialogue clarity, avoid intense flashing, and use properly licensed music, fonts, footage and overlays. Techniques are not guaranteed one-click presets; implementation varies by editor and version.
