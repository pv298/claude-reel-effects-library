# Reel editing workflow

1. Define one audience, one message and one primary action.
2. Confirm platform, aspect ratio and duration.
3. Organise original footage, voiceover, brand assets and licensed audio.
4. Build a story: hook → context → value/proof → payoff → CTA.
5. Remove pauses and repetition without changing meaning.
6. Choose a small number of motivated effects; do not decorate every cut.
7. Keep color consistent, skin tones natural and sound effects below dialogue.
8. Correct captions and keep text inside platform safe areas.
9. Review once muted, once audio-only and once on a phone.
10. Verify names, claims, CTA, licensing and export settings.
