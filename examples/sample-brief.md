# Example Reel edit brief

- Platform: Instagram Reels
- Format: 9:16 vertical
- Goal: Explain one practical marketing tip in under 30 seconds
- Audience: Small-business owners
- Tone: Clear, energetic, credible
- Sequence: Spoken hook → problem → two actionable steps → concise CTA
- Suggested techniques: Jump Cut, Word-by-Word Captions, Push-In, Highlight Sweep, Beat-Synced Cuts
- Guardrail: Preserve the speaker's meaning; use no unsupported performance claims.
